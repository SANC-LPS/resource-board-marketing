The way it works today

What labor scheduling costs you today

Nobody has the same picture

The schedule lives in a spreadsheet on one machine. Supers, PMs, and executives all work from their own copy, and none of them match by Wednesday.

Availability is guesswork

When a project needs four more people next week, finding them means calling supers one at a time. There's no single view of who rolls off when, so the answer depends on who you ask.

The schedule isn't shared

Assignments live in a spreadsheet or a project file on one machine. Everyone downstream works from a copy that's already out of date, and changes don't propagate.

What changes

One board. Everyone sees the same thing. Right now.

Every assignment, transfer, callback, and hour lives in one place. Change it once and it's changed for the foreman on site, the ops manager in the office, and the executive looking at next month.

Capabilities

Built around how self-perform work actually runs.

Request log

Every project team member with credentials can request manpower from the board. Each request is stamped with who asked, what they needed, when, and what happened to it. The person moving people stops being a bottleneck and starts working from a queue — and there's a permanent record of every decision.

Live resource board

Drag any person between projects and the board updates instantly for everyone. Color-coded by role, commitment, and availability. GC and self-perform crews in one view. See who rolls off when and who's available next, at a glance.

Company-wide labor forecasting

A rolling look-ahead across every active project, not one job at a time. See where demand exceeds the people you have, weeks before it becomes a phone call. One click copies it for company-wide distribution.

Scheduled and chain transfers

Set moves weeks ahead. Chain them so one person runs Project A to B to C automatically on the dates you set. Email and SMS reminders reach the employee 24 hours and 2 hours before shift start.

Digital timecards

Foremen submit crew time from their phone. OT and DT flag automatically. Admin reviews, approves, or returns with notes.

Permissions that hold

Owner, Administrator, Manager, Foreman, Viewer, with per-user overrides. Only admins grant permissions. Sensitive fields stay gated by role.

Works in the field

Full functionality on phone and tablet. Office and field always see the same data.

Who built this

Built by a Director of Field Operations

It came out of a real operation

Resource Board was built to run crews across multiple active projects at a self-perform contractor. Every feature exists because something in the existing process broke.

It replaced spreadsheet management

Assignments used to live in local spreadsheets that only one person could see and nobody trusted. Resource Board systemizes the same process and makes it visible company-wide, in real time.
